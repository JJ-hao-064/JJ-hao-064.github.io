About

I am Jinghao Jiang, a Master's student in Vehicle Engineering (Automotive Electronics) at Tongji University. My research focuses on intelligent battery management systems, especially State of Charge (SOC) and State of Health (SOH) estimation for lithium-ion batteries.

I am interested in applying deep learning models to battery time-series data and deploying them efficiently on resource-constrained embedded systems. My work covers the full pipeline from data preprocessing and model design to model compression, quantization, ONNX conversion, and embedded inference.

Research

My current research explores lightweight Transformer-based models for battery SOC estimation. I focus on modeling battery dynamics from multi-variable time-series signals, including voltage, current, and temperature, and improving the deployability of deep learning models through knowledge distillation and quantization.

I am particularly interested in bridging the gap between machine learning models and real-world automotive embedded systems, enabling reliable, efficient, and deployable AI solutions for safety-critical applications.

Projects

Lightweight Transformer for Battery SOC Estimation on Edge BMS
May 2025 - Present
  • Constructed SOC estimation samples from lithium-ion battery datasets using voltage, current, and temperature signals.
  • Designed a Transformer Encoder-based regression model for battery time-series modeling.
  • Built a lightweight student model using knowledge distillation and intermediate feature alignment.
  • Evaluated FP32 inference and INT8 quantization using ONNX Runtime, considering accuracy, model size, and CPU latency.
Battery SOH Estimation with Edge AI and MCU Deployment
Dec. 2024 - May 2025
  • Extracted health-related features from charging curves and performed feature engineering for SOH estimation.
  • Built an LSTM-based time-series prediction model and optimized hyperparameters using Bayesian optimization.
  • Converted the model to ONNX and deployed it to STM32H743 using X-CUBE-AI.
  • Evaluated embedded inference latency, memory footprint, and deployability.
  • Result: Conference paper accepted by CEVVE with oral presentation.
Vehicle Load Estimation Based on LSTM
May 2023 - May 2024
  • Developed and validated an LSTM-based vehicle load prediction model.
  • Result: Invention patent, "A Vehicle Load Estimation Method Based on LSTM Neural Network".
